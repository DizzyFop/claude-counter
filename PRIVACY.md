# Privacy Policy

Last updated October 25, 2026

Claude Counter runs only on claude.ai. It has no analytics or tracking, and it doesn't send your data to the developer or anyone else.

## What it reads

- The open conversation, from the data claude.ai loads for the page, so it can count tokens. After a branch switch it asks claude.ai for the conversation again.
- Your session and weekly usage, from claude.ai's usage endpoint and from the usage update claude.ai sends with each reply.
- The `lastActiveOrg` cookie, which holds your organization ID, so it knows where to ask for your usage.

## Where it goes

All of this is processed in your browser, inside the claude.ai tab. The extension only makes requests to claude.ai, with the session you're already signed in with. Nothing is sold, shared, or sent to any other server.

## What it stores

The two display settings from the toolbar popup, in the browser's extension storage on your device. Conversation text and usage numbers stay in memory while the tab is open and are gone when you close it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
